What is AIRism — and why does it work?

AIRism is Uniqlo's proprietary comfort conditioning technology, developed alongside Japanese textile manufacturer Toray and first launched in 2012. At its core, it uses ultra-fine polyester microfibers approximately 1/10th the width of a human hair. The structure of these fibers creates a capillary effect: moisture is drawn away from the skin and evaporated quickly, keeping the fabric — and you — feeling dry rather than damp.

AIRism dries approximately 1.9 times faster than cotton — meaning it wicks away sweat before your outfit has a chance to show it.

Source: Uniqlo official AIRism press communications, May 2025, distributed via the global AIRism Survey. Reported across multiple outlets including Manila Bulletin and Patches of Life.

Beyond quick-drying performance, AIRism also offers built-in odour neutralisation — the fibers absorb and counteract sources of sweat odour including ammonia, acetic acid, and nonenal. The result is fabric that actively manages the microclimate between your skin and your clothes, so you can move through a full day without thinking about it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Uniqlo AIRism worth it?

Yes — especially for summer. The quick-drying and odour-neutralising properties make a noticeable difference compared to standard cotton. The pieces are also priced accessibly, making it easy to build a full warm-weather wardrobe without breaking the budget.

What is the difference between AIRism and regular cotton?

Standard cotton absorbs moisture and holds it against your skin. AIRism uses ultra-fine polyester microfibers to wick moisture away and dry approximately 1.9 times faster than cotton, so the fabric stays light and dry rather than heavy and damp as the day goes on.

Where does the 1.9× drying claim come from?

The figure comes from Uniqlo's own official AIRism press communications released as part of the global AIRism Survey in May 2025, conducted with research firm Dynata across more than 7,000 participants in multiple countries. The claim has been reported by outlets including Manila Bulletin and Patches of Life.

Can AIRism be worn as outerwear?

Many AIRism pieces — including the T-shirts, bra tops, and shorts in this edit — are designed to be worn as standalone outerwear. The AIRism Cotton T-Shirts in particular have a clean cotton-look finish that reads as a normal tee rather than performance wear.

How do I wash AIRism clothing?

Most AIRism items are machine washable at up to 40°C on a gentle cycle. Always check the care label on your specific product. Do not tumble dry, as heat can damage the microfiber structure that makes the fabric work.
